rhythm games
overview
rhythm games have recently taken a profound center stage on my list of personal interests, particularly with my rediscovery of the genre and like, having too much free time on my hands.

this is a list of rhythm games that i currently mainly play, i do also list a small set of rhythm games that i do wish to play once more in the future.
vivid/stasis
personal best: 17.106 / class 9 (v5.0.0)
vivid/stasis is a indie mystery-rhythm game developed by team vividstasis (hajimeli) that honestly has a shockingly good story for it's price (please go into it blind i beg of you)

its free and honestly just try it, the gameplay is mainly 4k mania (with some bumper note and hurt note gimmicks), which makes it play very technically as you have to process what's the best way to actually hit the bumper notes instead of just directly sightreading the traditional notes

it just had it's full release recently (as time of writing this), so try it out!
Arcaea
personal best: ptt 10.06 (im not that good ok)
the funny mobile rhythm game with arcs and overhands, very fun game but certialy has a difficulty spike between 8+ -> 9 and 9 -> 9+

while it does have difficulty spikes, progression in terms of skill feels very visible, game is also somewhat f2p friendly with the content it gives (albeit most of the game is locked behind multiple paywalls)

the story exists, i havent fully explored into it much since f2p tho. i did start working on this game since in falsus is coming out sometime soon
Phigros + phira
personal best:rks 13.24
this rhythm game holds a warm place in my heart as the first rhythm game that i properly tried out on mobile, and i honestly still love it for it's very interesting charting methods

the main gimmick of this game is that everything can move; notes can move, and the judgement lines can too, which makes sightreading it a really fun challenge

this also lends itself to a very cool minimalistic-ish aesthetic where every chart styling is often just chart elements (except in the rare exception of story / anomaly unlocks)

i will say though that the story seems interesting, if only more of it was properly translated lol
